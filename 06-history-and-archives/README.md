# 06 - History and Archives

The site as it was, not as it is. Historical URLs and pages, past WHOIS and DNS, and links between sites that shared an analytics or ad ID.

## Subcategories

- [web-archive/](web-archive/) - Wayback CDX, waybackurls, gau - historical URLs and pages
- [ip-whois-history/](ip-whois-history/) - Historical WHOIS and passive DNS
- [analytics-linking/](analytics-linking/) - SpyOnWeb - sites sharing an analytics/ad ID
